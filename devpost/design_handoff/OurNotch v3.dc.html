Why each tab looks this way
Home — A bento of everything from your love: their latest photo gets the tall tile, their note sits beside it, and hours, weekends and live seconds sit underneath.
Note — Reads like a Messages reply: their latest note, a standard text field with a send circle, then a small segmented control for how it scrolls.
Emoji — A dedicated tab that says exactly what it does, six one-tap emoji, and one choice: out of the notch or full screen.
Photo — Send a new photo in one step; you see the last one you sent and whether it arrived.
