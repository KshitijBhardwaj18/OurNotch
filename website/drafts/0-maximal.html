Asked & answered

Does my partner need a Mac too?

Yes. OurNotch connects two Macs, so you'll both need it installed. Each Mac needs macOS 14 or later and has to be signed in to iCloud.

My Mac doesn't have a notch. Can I still use it?

Yes. On Macs without a notch, OurNotch shows a small black pill at the top of your screen, and it works the same way.

Can anyone else read our notes?

No. Everything is encrypted before it leaves your Mac, and only your two paired Macs have the key.

My iCloud storage is full. Will it still work?

Yes. OurNotch never stores anything in your personal iCloud storage, so a full iCloud makes no difference.

Will it get in the way while I'm working?

No. Closed, it's the same size as your notch and shows a small heart and your days count. It only opens when you hover over it, and it closes when you move away.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. OurNotch is Mac-only for now, built for couples who spend their days apart at their laptops.
