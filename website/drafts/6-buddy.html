FAQ

Before you download

Do we each need to buy it?

No. One license works for you both: your Mac and your person's.

What do we need?

Two Macs running macOS 14 or later, each signed in to iCloud.

Can anyone else read our notes?

No. Everything is end-to-end encrypted, so only your two paired Macs can read it.

My iCloud storage is full.

It still works. OurNotch doesn't use your iCloud storage.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

OurNotch shows a small black pill at the top of the screen and works the same way.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. OurNotch is Mac-only for now.
