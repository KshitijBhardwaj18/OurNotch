Questions

Do we each need to buy it?

No. One $8 purchase covers both of you: you on your Mac and your person on theirs.

Can anyone else read our notes?

No. Everything is end-to-end encrypted, so only your two paired Macs can read it.

My iCloud storage is full.

That's fine. OurNotch doesn't use your iCloud storage; it syncs through iCloud's public database. Both Macs do need to be signed in to iCloud.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

It still works. You get a small black pill at the top of the screen that does everything the notch does.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. OurNotch is Mac-only for now, and needs macOS 14 or later.
