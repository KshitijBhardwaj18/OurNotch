FAQ

Good questions.

Do we both need to buy it?

No. One license covers your Mac and your person's.

What do we need?

Two Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, each signed in to iCloud.

Can anyone else see what we send?

No. It's end-to-end encrypted, so only your two Macs can read it.

My iCloud storage is full. Will it work?

Yes. OurNotch doesn't use your iCloud storage.

What if my Mac has no notch?

OurNotch shows a small black pill at the top of the screen instead, and works the same way.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. OurNotch is Mac-only for now.
