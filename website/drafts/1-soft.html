Questions

Do we each need to buy it?

No. One $8 purchase covers both of you: you on your Mac and your person on theirs.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

That's fine. On Macs without a notch, OurNotch shows a small black pill at the top of the screen, and it works the same way.

Can anyone else read our notes?

No. Everything is encrypted before it leaves your Mac, and only your two paired Macs can read it.

My iCloud storage is full.

It still works. OurNotch never stores anything in your personal iCloud storage. Both Macs need macOS 14 or later and need to be signed in to iCloud.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. OurNotch is Mac-only for now.
