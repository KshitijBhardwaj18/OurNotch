06 / Questions06 / 06

A few questions.

The short version: one purchase, both Macs, and it stays between the two of you.

Do we each need to buy it?

No. One $8 purchase covers both of you: you on your Mac, and your person on theirs.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

That's fine. On Macs without a notch, OurNotch shows a small black pill at the top of the screen, and it works the same way.

Can anyone else read what we send?

No. It's end-to-end encrypted, so only your two paired Macs can read it.

My iCloud storage is full.

It still works. OurNotch syncs through iCloud's public database, not your own storage. Both Macs just need to be signed in to iCloud.

What do we need?

Two Macs on macOS 14 or later, both signed in to iCloud. No sign-up; you pair with a six-letter code.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. OurNotch is Mac-only for now.
